# Privacy policy

Remote for KEF doesn't collect personal data. It has no accounts, analytics or telemetry, and it sends nothing about you or how you use it to the developer or anyone else. Your settings, including the speakers you add, stay on your computer.

The app connects only to:

- **Your KEF speakers**, at the addresses you enter, over your local network, to read and change power, input, volume and playback.
- **GitHub**, to check for a new version and download it: once a day while automatic updates are on, and when you check by hand in Settings. Automatic updates can be turned off in Settings. These are plain requests for the release files, and on the Mac Sparkle's system profile is off. [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) applies to them.
- **The cover art address your speaker reports** for the current track, usually a streaming service's image server, to show the album cover.

Links in the app, such as the project page in Settings, open in your browser.
